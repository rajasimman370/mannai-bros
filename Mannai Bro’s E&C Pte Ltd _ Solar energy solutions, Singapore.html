About us

We were founded by practising professionals in building construction, with over 3 years of experience in electrical installations and building architecture. We apply the right solar technology to each client’s energy needs and architectural design.

Our key shareholders are qualified technical professionals, including an engineer and a registered technical officer. Together they aim to deliver solar solutions that work well and look good, so more building owners consider solar to replace or supplement their energy supply, and carbon emissions fall.

We believe everyone should do their part to slow global warming, however small. We want to leave a greener, more livable earth for our children.
